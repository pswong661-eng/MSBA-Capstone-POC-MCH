4Dashboard Notes

Evidence-boundary and governance points only. Full model metrics, baseline definitions, sign-convention mechanics and implementation options are documented in the report appendix.
  • Proof of concept — not for clinical use. Research demonstration for a future pilot; not production UI, not a clinical decision tool.
  • De-identified MIMIC-IV v3.1 research records. Not live MCH patient data. No PHI displayed.
  • Threshold 0.30 is a retrospective, capacity-oriented scenario — not a clinically validated MCH production threshold.
  • Medium and Low are prototype display tiers (0.15 boundary) for triage convenience only, not clinically validated categories. Only the 0.30 scenario is evaluated in the report.
  • Shapley values describe model behaviour only — not causal explanations, treatment recommendations, or evidence of clinical effectiveness.
  • The automated summary and review prompts are deterministic and rule-based — no language model, no external calls. They require human clinical review and are not treatment advice.
  • Missing labs use a −999 sentinel; admissions with no labs are flagged low-confidence.
  • Unit, service line and age-banded views are not yet available in this prototype.
  • Calibration and subgroup fairness metrics have not yet been computed and are required before any production recommendation (§8.1 of the report).
